# Roundhouse field drawings

Software Corporation (Hong Kong). The public page introduces app monitoring and authorized AI recovery. The workspace is for people operating their own applications.

## Landing

[Photon](https://photon.codes) uses a single page-scale composition; [Foglamp](https://foglamp.dev) puts concrete evidence next to brief copy. Use that economy without copying their marks or implying equivalent features. A typographic-only cover lost the connection to the name; a dark incident console made the example look like the product. The night shift scene is an original generated cutaway of a working engine house. Its approach signal, repair bays and dispatch office link to the corresponding monitoring, automatic recovery and reporting workflows.

The navy ground comes from the night scene. Amber identifies the workspace action. The incident record uses timestamps from a verified hosted fixture drill, not simulated live telemetry.

## Workspace

Use a horizontal navigation band, paper canvas and aligned tables. Use Archivo for headings and controls, and tabular digits for data. Keep endpoints, scheduler scope and per-action approval next to the controls they qualify. Scroll tables and navigation locally on narrow screens. Selection, disabled controls and errors must remain legible without relying on hue alone.

Owners see their connected apps. Guests work in an isolated example; drill controls and the example’s recovery policy belong together. Reportroom remains a separate synthetic reporting SaaS, not an owner navigation destination. Keep legal and deployment details in the repository and setup guide.
