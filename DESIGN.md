# Roundhouse field drawings

Software Corporation (Hong Kong). The public page introduces app monitoring and authorized AI recovery. The workspace is for people operating their own applications.

## Landing

[Photon](https://photon.codes) uses a single page-scale composition; [Foglamp](https://foglamp.dev) puts concrete evidence next to brief copy. Use that economy without copying their marks or implying equivalent features. A typographic-only cover lost the connection to the name; a dark incident console made the example look like the product. The architectural plate pairs a large, quiet Archivo heading with an original overhead engine-shed drawing. The seven-bay mark simplifies the thirteen-bay drawing for small sizes. Both are SVG paths, with no external imagery.

Use apricot (#f6c29e) for the drawing ground and blue-black (#203a49) for structural ink. The recovery record reverses the contrast. Its four entries describe a recorded fixture run, not live telemetry. The directory links into the actual workspace.

## Workspace

Use a horizontal navigation band, paper canvas and aligned tables. Serif page titles distinguish the current register from its controls; Archivo and tabular digits carry data. Keep endpoints, scheduler scope and per-action approval next to the controls they qualify. Scroll tables and navigation locally on narrow screens. Selection, disabled controls and errors must remain legible without relying on hue alone.

Owners see their connected apps. Guests work in an isolated example; drill controls and the example’s recovery policy belong together. Reportroom remains a separate synthetic reporting SaaS, not an owner navigation destination. Keep legal and deployment details in the repository and setup guide.
