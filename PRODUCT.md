# Roundhouse

## Platform
Web. Cloudflare Workers, D1 and Durable Objects; Rotary is the agent runtime.

## Purpose
Monitor configured systems continuously. Investigate failures, execute permitted recovery and independently verify health. New configurations run automatically; human approval is optional. Existing explicit approval policies remain explicit.

## Constraints
Authentication, tenant isolation, exact target/action authority and spending/request limits remain enforced. Owners connect their own public HTTPS apps; Reportroom is the example, not the platform's scope. Guests remain fixture-only and cannot connect external systems or invoke paid providers. No visitor tracking without opt-in.

## Reportroom
A reporting SaaS example: isolated demo workspace, synthetic shop orders, configurable asynchronous CSV reports, report history and saved workspace preferences. Reporting works without a recovery drill. No real billing or third-party data integrations.
