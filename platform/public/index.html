CONTEXT → INTENT → APPROVAL

More than a dashboard.
Less than a blank check.

Connect an agent over MCP. Let it read evidence and request bounded actions. The approval boundary stays outside the agent.

01 / CONNECT
⌘

An agent-native interface.

Manual, scoped bearer access at /mcp. Issue read, ingest, or control keys for the job at hand. No OAuth setup implied. An agent cannot approve its own action.

02 / UNDERSTAND
≋

Evidence, in one place.

Inspect bounded pageview and event counts, paths and referrers, recent service logs, health probes, and incident history. Not a full analytics or observability suite.

03 / DECIDE
↳

Control you can inspect.

Start or stop monitoring. Investigate an incident. Approve a pending recovery explicitly. Review what happened, then publish a status update when you choose.
